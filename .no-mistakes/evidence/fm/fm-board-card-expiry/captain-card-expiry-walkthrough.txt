================================================================

was its backing backlog item reporting `done`. An id with no backing item can

CRITERION 6 - the expiry window is one named constant, stated in the header
================================================================
  `add` records backlog_backed as true, false, or null when the backlog cannot
  be read. False, null, and legacy records without the field expire after
  UNBACKED_CARD_EXPIRY_DAYS (7) days without dropping their content.

The only place the number lives:
  92:UNBACKED_CARD_EXPIRY_DAYS=7
  93:UNBACKED_CARD_EXPIRY_SECONDS=$((UNBACKED_CARD_EXPIRY_DAYS * 24 * 60 * 60))
