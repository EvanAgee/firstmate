================================================================
 firstmate issue #127 -- scheduled route refresh under launchd
================================================================

 Setup, identical on both sides:
   * quota-axi is HEALTHY the whole time (reports 42% remaining).
   * It is installed at /private/tmp/fm127-demo/nvm-like/bin,
     standing in for a real ~/.nvm/versions/node/*/bin location.
   * launchd runs the job with its own minimal built-in PATH,
     which never includes that directory.
   * launchctl is faked; the real machine's launchd is untouched.
