Steering an OMP worker: does firstmate know the message landed?
===============================================================

This drives the real send path (fm_backend_tmux_send_text_submit ->
fm_tmux_submit_core) against a real tmux pane running a real
`bun <omp-entry>` process whose input box accepts typing and clears on
Enter. The printed verdict IS the delivery confirmation.

OMP worker process in the pane: /Users/evanagee/.bun/bin/bun /tmp/omp-e2e/fake-omp-tty.js

steering the OMP worker with: "switch to the smaller model"

delivery verdict from firstmate: unknown
  -> firstmate could NOT confirm delivery; it fails closed and refuses.
